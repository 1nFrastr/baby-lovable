# Personal site

Common map: **Home (brand hero)** → Work / Projects → About → Contact. One purpose per section; do not pack a dashboard into the landing page.

## Hero

Brand or name as the dominant signal. One headline, one supporting sentence, one CTA (View work / Contact). Full-bleed portrait, craft photo, or atmospheric visual — not a card collage.

## Work

Show 3–6 projects with title, role, one-line outcome, and a link or detail page. Prefer a strong list or editorial layout over identical rounded cards with soft shadows.

## About & contact

Short bio in the subject's voice. Contact: one form or mailto/links — no fake analytics widgets. Optional: simple blog index later, not in v1 unless asked.

## Tone

Match the person's craft (designer ≠ engineer ≠ chef). Pair with `frontend-design` for tokens and motion restraint.
